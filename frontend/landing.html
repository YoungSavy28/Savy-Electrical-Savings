About

A first-pass takeoff in minutes, not an afternoon.

Savy Electrical Estimates is Plan Takeoff: a tool that reads an electrical plan the way an estimator does. It starts with the plan's own legend, finds every symbol on the drawing, follows the dashed switch-control lines, and prices the job for that specific house. A person reviews every count before it goes anywhere.

1

Upload any plan

PNG, JPG or PDF. Every plan has its own legend, so the tool reads that one instead of assuming symbols.

2

Symbols found and counted

Receptacles, GFCIs, switches, lights, smoke alarms, fans and panels are matched to the legend, then checked by rules so letters in room names are not counted as switches.

3

Wire runs traced

The dashed switch-control lines are followed from switch to light and measured in feet once you set the scale. Draw a run by hand if one is wrong.

4

You review, then it prices

Click any box to retype or remove it. When the legend mapping looks right, the app searches the web for current prices of exactly the parts that plan needs, and totals materials, labor and contingency.
